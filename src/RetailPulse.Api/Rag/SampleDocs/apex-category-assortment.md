# Apex Retail Group — Category & Assortment Reference (Sample)

> **Sample corpus notice.** This document is a fictional grounding artifact attributed to the sample tenant "Apex Retail Group." Category segmentation, review cadences, and thresholds below are illustrative and not benchmarks.

## 1. Category Roles

Every Apex category is assigned exactly one role at each banner. Roles are reviewed twice a fiscal year:

| Role | Purpose | Typical Depth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destination | Draws banner-defining trips. | Full depth, exclusive lines allowed. |
| Routine | Fills recurring baskets. | Moderate depth, competitive parity. |
| Occasion | Anchors gifting or seasonal moments. | Depth ramps with the seasonal window. |
| Fill-In | Convenience adjacencies to core baskets. | Narrow, curated, no long-tail. |

A category cannot hold two roles at the same banner at the same time. A Ridgeline Bourbon anchor at a spirits banner can be Destination; at a grocery banner the same brand may play a Fill-In role.

## 2. Assortment Review Cycle

Assortment reviews follow a documented eight-step flow:

1. Category role confirmation.
2. Consumer demand signals from the demand forecast pod.
3. Distributor and supplier service posture (see the supplier service levels reference).
4. Planogram feasibility with the current Shelf-Set Wave.
5. Margin architecture — Good/Better/Best tier gaps.
6. Innovation slot analysis — how many new SKUs the assortment can absorb.
7. Rationalization candidates — SKUs below the banner's velocity floor.
8. Portfolio depletion check on affected suppliers.

Reviews close with a decision packet stamped by the category manager and the banner merchandising lead.

## 3. Assortment Guardrails

Apex enforces four assortment guardrails during a review:

- **Anchor Retention.** Anchors identified in the planogram reference stay in the assortment unless a rationalization case is documented and countersigned by the banner merchandising lead.
- **Regional Exception Register.** Region-specific SKUs (for example West Coast citrus mixers) live in the exception register and are re-validated every review.
- **Innovation Cap.** A single review cannot slot more than two new SKUs per linear foot of shelf.
- **Depletion Guard.** Rationalization decisions require a supplier depletion review so the exit does not strand contract volume.

## 4. Rationalization Signals

SKUs enter rationalization scrutiny when any two of the following hold for two consecutive review periods:

- Velocity below the banner's category-level floor.
- Zero incremental basket association (no meaningful attach behavior).
- Persistent void flags from the planogram reference.
- Sustained margin below the banner category floor.

A single soft signal is a note, not a rationalization case. Rationalization cases are treated as reversible for two review periods so the category can be recovered if the exit hurts the assortment.

## 5. Vocabulary

- **Anchor** — the top-velocity SKUs a category keeps in prime shelf positions.
- **Category Role** — the strategic role the category plays for a banner.
- **Innovation Slot** — the reserved capacity for new SKUs during a review.
- **Depletion Guard** — the supplier-side check performed before delisting a SKU.
