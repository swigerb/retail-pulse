# Halcyon Pet Supply — Nutrition Standards Reference (Sample)

> Sample corpus notice. This document is a fictional grounding artifact for the Halcyon Pet Supply Co. pack. Brand names, life-stage cadences, and thresholds below are illustrative and not benchmarks.

## Life-Stage Framework

Halcyon organizes companion-food assortment across four life stages: Puppy, Adult, Senior, and Weight Management. Every nutrition SKU is tagged to one life stage; multi-stage claims are not permitted on the pack shelf. A household typically transitions its pet through 2-3 stages over the customer relationship — the auto-ship signal fires when refill cadence stretches past its historical median for a life-stage bucket, indicating a transition candidate.

## Grain-Inclusive vs. Grain-Free Mix

Regional mix targets are set per quarter. Sunbelt runs a higher grain-free share due to the summer sensitivity pattern; Great Lakes runs a higher grain-inclusive share due to winter caloric needs. A store deviating from its regional target by more than eight percentage points for two consecutive weeks triggers a merchandising review.

## Auto-Ship Cadence

Auto-ship refill windows are anchored to the SKU's average daily feed rate. When a household's actual cadence stretches past 120 percent of the projected refill date for a life-stage-appropriate SKU, the account is flagged for a nutrition-planner outreach: either a life-stage transition, a household composition change, or an at-risk churn signal. Cadence contractions past 80 percent typically indicate a second pet in the household — a cross-sell opening.

## Vocabulary

- **Refill cadence** — the interval between successive auto-ship deliveries of the same SKU to the same household.
- **Transition candidate** — a household whose auto-ship signal suggests moving from one life stage to the next.
- **Regional mix target** — the quarterly grain-inclusive vs. grain-free share goal per region.
- **Cross-sell opening** — a cadence contraction that indicates household growth.
