# Halcyon Pet Supply — Grooming Bar Service Playbook (Sample)

> Sample corpus notice. This document is a fictional grounding artifact for the Halcyon Pet Supply Co. pack. Booking cadences, service SKUs, and utilization thresholds below are illustrative and not benchmarks.

## Service Menu

The Halcyon Grooming Bar offers four service SKUs: Bath, Full Groom, Nail Trim, and De-Shed. Each is booked in a fixed appointment slot (Bath 45 minutes, Full Groom 90 minutes, Nail Trim 15 minutes, De-Shed 60 minutes). Weekends run at 1.6x weekday capacity because of the neighborhood-store foot-traffic pattern.

## Utilization Metrics

Booking utilization is the ratio of booked slots to available slots per store per day. A store dropping below 62 percent for a full week enters coaching review. No-show attrition is measured as booked-and-uncancelled appointments that failed to arrive; the platform-wide floor is 8 percent, and any store above 12 percent for two consecutive weeks triggers a scheduling process review.

## Attach Behaviour

Grooming visits attach to food and enrichment refills at a strong rate — a household visiting the Grooming Bar in a given week completes a food or wellness refill in the same visit at roughly a 3-to-1 ratio compared with a curbside-only visit. Storefront layouts are designed so the Grooming Bar waiting area is adjacent to wellness bays for exactly this reason.

## Escalations

A grooming appointment that fails a satisfaction check (owner rating below 3/5) opens a service-recovery task. Tasks age out at 72 hours; ageing tasks escalate to the district service manager. Coaching flags never auto-close — the grooming lead has to sign off.

## Vocabulary

- **Slot utilization** — booked minutes divided by scheduled bar minutes.
- **No-show attrition** — the share of booked appointments that failed to arrive.
- **Attach behaviour** — the tendency of a grooming visit to trigger a same-visit food or wellness refill.
- **Service-recovery task** — the follow-up workflow after a low-satisfaction visit.
