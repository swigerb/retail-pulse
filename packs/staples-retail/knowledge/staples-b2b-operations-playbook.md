# Staples (Unofficial Demo) — B2B Operations Playbook (Sample)

> Disclosure: unofficial demonstration with no affiliation with Staples. All figures and rules are synthetic and illustrative; trademarks belong to their owners.

## Contract Catalog and Budgets

Each synthetic business account has a contract catalog price tier and a quarterly office-supply budget. Budget burn above 90 percent of plan before the last month of the quarter is flagged for account review. Example: a synthetic account with a 40,000-dollar budget and 37,000 dollars spent at week 9 of 13 is at 92.5 percent burn.

## Fulfillment and Print Orders

Service level is measured as on-time, in-full delivery; the synthetic target is 95 percent. Days of supply below 7 for a contract item triggers a substitution review. Print orders move through Received, Proof Approved, In Production, Shipped; a print order is late-risk when proof approval is more than two days old.

## Substitutions

A substitution is allowed only when the account's approved list names the replacement item. Unapproved substitutions must be escalated, not shipped.

## Quote Approval

Quotes valued over 25,000 synthetic dollars, or with discount beyond the contract tier, require human approval. No quote is treated as sent until approval is recorded.

## Vocabulary

- **Sell-through** — units sold divided by units available in a period.
- **On-shelf availability** — share of items in stock at a location.
- **Days of supply** — on-hand units divided by average daily units.
