# Prairiehearth Craft Supply — Workshop Programming Reference (Sample)

> Sample corpus notice. This document is a fictional grounding artifact for the Prairiehearth Craft Supply Co. pack. Session lengths, seat targets, and attach thresholds below are illustrative and not benchmarks.

## Programming Calendar

Prairiehearth workshop stores run a rolling six-week programming calendar organized by season: fibre in autumn, paper journaling in winter, paint plein-air in spring, and woodcraft outdoor bench work in summer. Every store commits to at least four sessions per week; larger workshop-anchor stores commit to eight.

## Session Structure

Each session is one of three tiers: Beginner (90 minutes, 8-seat cap), Intermediate (2 hours, 6-seat cap), and Advanced (3 hours, 4-seat cap). Seat cap is the ceiling — nominal enrollment target is 75 percent of the cap for beginner sessions and 85 percent for intermediate and advanced.

## Kit Attach

A workshop session is not merely revenue — it is the single biggest kit-attach driver in the store. Every registered seat has an associated kit SKU or kit-bundle. The attach rate is the share of registered seats that redeem the associated kit at the storefront within seven days of the session. The platform floor is 82 percent; a store below 72 percent for two consecutive sessions enters a programming review.

## Regional Overlay

Seasonal peaks vary by region. Prairie Belt runs a longer autumn fibre window because of local guild activity; Coastal Cottages compresses winter paper journaling into a shorter, denser calendar to accommodate the tourist off-season traffic pattern. Foothills stores lean heavily on woodcraft joinery.

## Vocabulary

- **Session tier** — the difficulty band a workshop is scheduled at (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
- **Seat cap** — the maximum number of makers a session can accommodate.
- **Enrollment target** — the store's expected fill rate against the seat cap.
- **Kit attach** — the share of registered seats that redeem their kit within seven days.
- **Programming review** — the coaching workflow triggered when kit attach falls below the review floor.
