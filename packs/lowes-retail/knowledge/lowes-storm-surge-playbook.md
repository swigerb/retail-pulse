# Storm Surge Continuity Playbook (Synthetic)

> Unofficial demonstration. No affiliation with Lowe's. Synthetic operational data; trademarks belong to their owners.

## Demand Signals
Storm Readiness sales rise as a forecast cone narrows. Synthetic planning thresholds: generator units sold per store per day above 6 for two days triggers a replenishment review; plywood on-shelf availability below 92% triggers a transfer request between Regional Distribution Centers.

## Days of Supply
Days of supply = on-hand units divided by trailing three-day average daily units. Below 2.0 is critical, 2.0 to 4.0 is watch, above 4.0 is healthy.

## Substitutions and Fulfillment
When a generator size is out of stock, suggest the next wattage tier only after confirming the customer's stated load. Order Pickup and Home Delivery are paused before Pro Desk orders when capacity is constrained.

## Safety and Approval
Any customer-facing safety guidance (generator placement, carbon monoxide, fuel storage) must be reviewed by a human approver before publication. Agents draft; people approve.
